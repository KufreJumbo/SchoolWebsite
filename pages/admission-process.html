Admissions

A simple beginning

Start your child’s Sagab journey.

Sagab Schools admits boys and girls into Sagab Montessori School (Pre-School to Grade 6) and Sagab College (JSS 1 to SSS 3) in Solomade Estate, Ikorodu, Lagos. Admission takes four clear steps:

EnquireCall or email us, or book a school visit.
ApplyObtain and complete the application form from the school.
AssessmentAn age-appropriate entrance test and a short interview.
WelcomeReceive your offer, pay fees and resume.

Questions parents ask

Admissions questions, answered.

Can’t find your answer here? Our admissions team is happy to help.

Who can apply?

Boys and girls joining pre-school, primary or secondary school, including children transferring from other schools. Transfer students should bring a transfer letter and their last school report.

Is there an entrance examination?

Yes. Every applicant sits an age-appropriate entrance test and a short interview, so we can place your child in the right class. Contact admissions for the next assessment date.

Can my child board?

Boarding is available for Sagab College students, with house staff, supervised evening prep and daily meals. Hostel life

How much are the fees?

See school fees for each class, what the fees include and how to pay. Payment plans are available.

Can we visit the school?

Yes. Call admissions to arrange a visit to our schools in Solomade Estate, Ikorodu.
