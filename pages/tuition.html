Questions parents ask

School fees questions, answered.

Need a breakdown for a specific class? Call the school on +234 706 629 7062.

How much are Sagab school fees?

Montessori fees range from ₦70,000 per term for Pre-School to ₦92,000 per term for Grade 1 – 6. Secondary tuition ranges from ₦62,000 for JSS 1 to ₦69,000 for SSS 3, plus compulsory charges and items.

What do secondary fees include?

Besides tuition, secondary students pay consolidated charges of ₦15,700 covering medical, examination and caution fees, PTA, the development levy and an online result checker. Uniform, track suit, ID card, registration and other compulsory items are listed in the table for each class.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. Contact the school bursary or admissions office to discuss a payment plan.

How much is boarding?

Boarding fees are not included in the totals above. Contact school management for current hostel fees, which are paid into the Fidelity Bank account. Hostel life

Are there optional fees?

Coding and Robotics is an optional programme for secondary students at ₦10,000, paid into the Stanbic IBTC account.

How do I pay?

Pay by bank transfer into the official Sagab College account for each purpose, listed under Payment Channels above. See admissions if you are applying for the first time.
