A connected journey

Learning that grows with every child.

Sagab Schools offers a complete education in Ikorodu, Lagos: pre-school and primary at Sagab Montessori School, then junior and senior secondary at Sagab College.

Every stage combines strong foundations, practical learning and high expectations, and prepares students for the JSCE, WASSCE and NECO examinations.

Exams & student development

Prepared for exams, and for life.

Academic support and personal development go hand in hand at every stage of a Sagab education.

Examination preparation

Sagab College has presented JSCE candidates since 2007 and WASSCE candidates since 2010, and is accredited by NECO. Students prepare through consistent practice, laboratory practicals and close academic support, and sit external papers in our WAEC-approved examination hall.
