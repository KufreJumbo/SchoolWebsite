From our founder

Every child deserves to be seen, valued and empowered.

Dear Parents, Guardians, and Students,

Welcome to Sagab Schools — a place where every child is seen, valued, and empowered to become the very best version of themselves.

When I founded this institution, I carried a single, unwavering conviction: that quality education is not a privilege reserved for the few, but a right that every child deserves. That conviction has guided every decision we have made, every teacher we have hired, and every programme we have designed.

At Sagab, we do not simply teach subjects — we shape character. We nurture curiosity, build resilience, and instil the values of integrity, compassion, and excellence. Our students leave our halls not only with strong academic foundations but with the confidence and moral clarity to lead in a rapidly changing world.

To our parents and guardians: thank you for entrusting us with your most precious gift. We do not take that responsibility lightly. We are committed to maintaining open, honest communication with every family, because we believe that the partnership between home and school is the single greatest predictor of a child's success.

To our students: you are the reason we are here. Dream boldly. Work diligently. Treat others with kindness. And know that every member of this school community is cheering for you.

The journey ahead is full of possibility. I invite you to walk it with us.

With warmth and purpose,

Dr. Mrs. Beauty Obodo

Proprietress, Sagab Schools
