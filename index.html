Welcome to Sagab

Where excellence becomes a daily habit.

Founded in 2005, Sagab Schools brings Sagab Montessori School and Sagab College together on neighbouring sites in Solomade Estate, Ikorodu. We create the conditions for every learner to think deeply, act responsibly, and approach the future with confidence, from a strong Montessori foundation to an ambitious college experience.
